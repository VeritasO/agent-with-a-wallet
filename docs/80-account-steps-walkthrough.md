Do them in this order — each one feeds the next.

## 1. Devpost account + hackathon registration (~5 min)
- Sign up at devpost.com with your own account (you are the entrant of record).
- Find "Build What's Next with PayPal and AI" and register for it.
- Nothing to fill in about the project yet — Nyx drafts the submission text;
  you review it at the end.

## 2. PayPal developer account + sandbox app (~10 min)
- Sign up / sign in at developer.paypal.com with your PayPal account.
- **If it asks to verify by code: choose the VOICE CALL option.**
  Automated SMS codes do not reach your phone — that is a known fact about
  your plan, not a problem with the account.
- Create an app in the **Sandbox** (not Live). Name is free — e.g. "Nyx Treasury Demo".
- This produces two values: a sandbox **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.
- **Hold on to them — do not email them anywhere.** When you reach this step,
  tell Nyx you're there and she'll walk the handover the safe way.
- Sandbox only: no real money is involved at any point in this entry.

## 3. Public repo host (~5 min)

  your account (GitHub is the default route).
- If you already have a GitHub account, nothing to create — just tell Nyx.
- If not: create one, and again, voice call over SMS if a code is offered.

## 4. YouTube (~2 min)
